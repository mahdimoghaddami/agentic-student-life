# Evaluation case selection

Twenty fixed participant/time cases cover eight StudentLife identifiers. Selection prioritizes useful sensing/EMA coverage and deliberately includes low-stress, missing-observation and longitudinal preference scenarios; it is not random population sampling. All decisions are at 20:00 in America/New_York.

Goals, coaching preferences, constraints, recent-interaction notes and scripted feedback are explicitly simulated. Profiles include separately labeled pre-term survey background. Feedback is released after the current action. Expected actions are manually allowed offline labels, withheld from agents and evaluators.

Cases 009 and 010 fall after the selected participants' passive recording ended on May 31; they test post-recording absence. Cases 011 and 012 apply labeled synthetic modality masks. Cases 013-015 and 016-018 form the two three-step preference episodes.
