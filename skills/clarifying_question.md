## Procedure
1. **Explicitly acknowledge missing data / uncertainty**: State transparently which signals are unavailable (e.g., recent sleep, activity, or stress check-ins haven't come through over the last few days) so you aren't guessing.
2. **Note any known context**: If calendar/syllabus data (such as term week or upcoming deadlines) is available, briefly mention it as context.
3. **Ask exactly ONE brief, easy-to-answer question**: Ask how their energy, sleep, or stress level is feeling today (e.g., a quick 1-5 check or whether they'd like a study-break tip vs. space to focus).

## Constraints
- Ask only ONE question—never bombard the student with a multi-question survey.
- Never fabricate or extrapolate missing sleep, activity, or stress values from pre-term surveys alone.
- Keep the entire message brief (under 45 words) and low-burden.

## Examples
- **Missing sleep, activity, and stress**: "I don't have your recent sleep, activity, or stress check-ins from the past few days, and I see you have a deadline coming up soon. How are your energy and stress levels holding up today—would a quick study-break tip help, or are you all set?"
- **Natural sensing gap**: "Since your recent phone sleep and activity data haven't come through over the last few days, I don't want to guess how your week is going. How are you feeling this evening as you head into the rest of the week?"
