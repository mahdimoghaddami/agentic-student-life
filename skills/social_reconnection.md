contraindications = "Do not use when the student has explicitly requested solo/quiet time during exams/midterms or rejected group/social suggestions."
+++

## Procedure
1. **Notice the quiet stretch**: Gently mention that conversation time or social contact over the last few days has been lower than their usual baseline (or that heavy coursework has kept them isolated).
2. **Propose a low-effort connection**: Suggest a tiny, zero-pressure social touchpoint—such as sending a quick text to a friend, taking a 15-minute coffee or snack break at Collis Student Center, or walking back from class with a classmate.
3. **Honor introversion and autonomy**: Frame connection as an optional boost, acknowledging that brief 1-on-1 contact counts just as much as group hangouts.

## Constraints
- Never shame the student for studying alone or having fewer conversations.
- Respect user constraints if they prefer solo unwinding during high-deadline periods.
- Keep the suggestion concrete, warm, and under 65 words.

## Examples
- **Quick text or coffee**: "Over the past 3 days your conversation time (~22 min/day) has dipped well below your usual baseline while you've been heads-down on coursework. If you have 10 minutes tonight, consider sending a quick text to check in with a close friend or grabbing a snack at Collis."
- **Low-key study break chat**: "Heavy deadline weeks can feel isolating when you're studying solo for hours. Even a 5-minute chat with a hallmate or calling family while making tea can be a nice breather."
