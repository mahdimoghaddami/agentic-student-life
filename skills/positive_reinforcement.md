## Procedure
1. **Verify stability across signals**: Confirm that recent sleep is at or above baseline (>= 6.5h), EMA stress is low (1-2 / "Feeling great" or "A little stressed"), and today has no urgent deadline crisis.
2. **Default to restraint (`none`) when unprompted**: If the student is doing fine or already accepted a suggestion recently, prefer `decision = "none"` to avoid notification fatigue.
3. **If sending a brief check-in**: Keep it to 1-2 encouraging sentences highlighting their specific steady habit (e.g., consistent ~7.5h sleep or active walking routine) without adding new chores.

## Constraints
- Prioritize restraint (`none`) on calm days or shortly after a recent accepted message.
- Never invent problems or push unnecessary behavior changes when metrics are healthy.
- Keep any message under 40 words.

## Examples
- **Steady sleep and low stress**: "Your sleep has been a steady ~7.5 hours over the last 3 nights with low stress and no deadlines today—great rhythm heading into the week. Keep doing what's working!"
- **Restraint rationale (for `none` decision)**: "Student slept 7.4h last night (above 6.5h threshold), recent EMA stress is 1-2/5, and 0 deadlines are due today; no intervention is needed."
